Napspot privacy policy

Draft, last updated 2026-10-22. Not legal advice: have it reviewed before launch.

What Napspot collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no ads. Your progress, settings and best times are stored only on your device.

This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from other companies. Like any website, the host that serves it sees visitors' IP addresses in its server logs.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, the daily leaderboard sends your daily solve time to Apple's Game Center, under your Game Center name. Apple's privacy policy covers that data. You can sign out, or hide your name, in the Settings app under Game Center.

Purchases

The optional "All levels" purchase is handled by Apple. We don't receive your payment details.

Children

Napspot doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
