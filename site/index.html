Puzzles should be calm

Too many puzzle apps break your concentration with an ad after nearly every level, then ask you to watch another one for a hint. Napspot has neither.

How it works

1

Rule spots out

Tap a spot once to mark where a cat can't go.

2

Place a cat

Tap again, or double tap, to place a cat.

3

Give everyone space

One per row, column and colour, none touching. Three slips and you start fresh.

What you get

  • No ads

    Not between levels, not for hints, not ever.

  • Hints you earn

    Three to start, and one more for every daily board you finish.

  • One board a day

    The same board for everyone, with today's fastest times on Game Center.

  • 300 levels

    From 5×5 to 10×10, each with exactly one answer you can reach by logic.

  • Region shapes

    An optional shape in each colour, for players who can't tell the colours apart.

  • Plays offline

    Your progress stays on your phone. Nothing to sign up for.
